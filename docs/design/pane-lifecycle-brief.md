# Pane contract + lifecycle voice — confirmed brief

Source: owner shape interview 2026-08-22 (this file is the durable record;
supersedes the boxed-amber-badge treatment pending the wave that lands it).
Precedent format: `form-wave-brief.md`.

## Rulings (owner, 2026-08-22)

1. **Lifecycle mark: the gray ENTWURF word.** Quiet mono-meta text in
   `--on-surface-variant` — no amber, no box, no badge chrome. "Good enough
   for now." One treatment for every surface that shows lifecycle:
   - ledger: rides the Titel as today, recolored gray (amber leaves the
     ledger);
   - pane header, detail header, edit header: the same gray word replaces
     the boxed amber badge.
   Published renders nothing (absence = published — unchanged law).
   Cue-register consequence: row 4 (the one amber mark) is superseded by
   this treatment; no register row licenses amber lifecycle marks anymore.
   `--draft`/`--on-draft` PARK in tokens.css with a comment reserving them
   as the future Eingereicht/Submission lifecycle channel (owner
   2026-08-22 — post-v1; not deleted, not licensed).
2. **Pane contract: scent.** The pulled sheet is a PREVIEW — identity
   (Signatur tab, Titel, gray ENTWURF when draft), media scent, a few key
   facts. Details live on the Article's own page. Composition may flex
   with content and available space ("it kind of depends on the content
   and the available space") — the pane adapts to its container, but its
   job never grows into the full reader. The pane and the detail page are
   no longer one view in two compositions: the pane previews, the detail
   page reads.
3. **Ledger keeps a quiet draft cue** (ruled earlier same day) — the gray
   ENTWURF word satisfies this; finding drafts is not delegated to a
   filter alone.

## Consequences

- **Issue B (pane omits lifecycle)** → implementable: pane header carries
  the gray ENTWURF word when the article is a draft (view-model gains the
  draft fact).
- **Issue C (Vorschau near-no-op at 512–1280px)** → settled by the scent
  contract: where the pane column is hidden, the Vorschau action targets
  the detail route (details on their own page); no pane at narrow widths.
- **Pane actions:** Öffnen is the primary act (go read the details).
  Whether Bearbeiten stays on the preview sheet is an OPEN decision for
  the wave's gate — default: keep it (archivist ergonomics, one click to
  the frequent act), remove only on an owner verdict.
- **Pane fact set:** which "few key facts" the preview shows is decided at
  the design gate on renders, not invented in code. Floor: Signatur,
  Titel, Datierung, media scent, lifecycle. The full Akte key/value list
  belongs to the detail page.

## Anti-goals

- No badge revival, no amber lifecycle mark, no new box chrome.
- No second dashed meaning (dashed stays "ohne Signatur" absence only).
- No global re-inking of published Signaturen — they render exactly as
  today.
- The pane does not grow toward the full reader; scope creep there is a
  defect, not initiative.
