# R2 Schaufenster — light first, dark finished

Thesis: the start page opens on the archive's content, the way a museum collection site does. A curated lead piece is shown at large scale, and the index (Bestände, Zeitleiste, Zuletzt) sits below as a quiet directory.

Structural difference: what leads is one object: a large media frame and a big serif title. It is not a search hero, a stack of sections or a table of contents. Search lives only in the slim header, on every page. The index sits under a left label rail ("Stöbern", "Zuletzt hinzugefügt"). archiv.html states its filters as one editable sentence ("Bestand Gruppen des DPB × · alle Jahrzehnte · jeder Typ") instead of chips. artikel.html is an object page: media large on the left, title and facts on the right (media first on phone), and onward lists by Bestand and by decade.

Tokens (light / dark): ground #fff / #0d0d0d · ink #0a0a0a / #f2f2f2 · ink-2 #5c5c5c / #a3a3a3 (text only) · rule #d6d6d6 / #3a3a3a · fill-inverse = ink (current page only) · error #b3261e / #ff6b61. Serif (ui-serif) is used for the wordmark and titles; body text is the system sans. Media placeholders are hairline frames with diagonals and a type label. There are no fills and no fake photos. Entwurf is an outlined uppercase mark with an italic title.

Judge: (1) Does a content-led start page (lead piece + 3 pieces, marked "später · Beispiel") orient members better than a search-first page? (2) The filter sentence: set values are ink and underlined with ×, open slots are gray and dotted. (3) The onward lists on artikel (Bestand | 1960er, small frames): do they carry link-holders into the archive?

## Cost/gain (kept against SCREEN-JOBS)
- Search is not "first and largest" on start. It sits in the slim header (widened to 24rem), because this direction tests content-led entry. If members reach for search first, this direction loses.
- Highlights lead is kept at large scale as the marked idea. The table rates it high gain once curated. It shows real entries and a labeled example text.
- Zuletzt hinzugefügt stays as 5 short lines. In the real app it hides while every date is the import date.
- The small counts next to Bestände and decades are kept (ink-2, small) for archivists ("Unsortiert 196"). The filter sentence stays at a moderate serif size. It is the active-filter display, not a page heading.
- Not built: bulk checkboxes and row actions, which are archivist-only on hover. Known leftovers from the one re-shoot: the drafts strip's top rule runs past the gutter on desktop. On phone, the secondary pieces' meta line wraps under the thumbnail, and the filter × can end up alone at a line end.
